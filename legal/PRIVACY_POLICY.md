# Lumolo Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** with Lumolo 1.1 (previous version: 23 November 2026) · **Operator:** Samofy Limited, 182-184 High Street North, East Ham, London E6 2JA, United Kingdom ·
**Contact:** support@samofy.co.uk

## 1. Summary
Lumolo is a video app for children that shows videos from a list of YouTube channels
checked by Lumolo before they are added. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising
of our own and no tracking across apps or websites. **We do not sell personal information or
use it for advertising.** The only data that can leave the device is optional **usage
statistics**, which are off until a parent turns them on (section 2).

## 2. Information we collect
Lumolo has no user accounts and does not ask for emails, photos, location, contacts or
advertising identifiers. A parent may type a nickname for each child so children can tell
their profiles apart; it is optional and stays on the device (section 3).

**Usage statistics (optional, off by default).** During setup, and at any time in
**Grown-ups → Share usage statistics**, a parent can choose to share usage statistics. Until
a parent turns this on, Lumolo does not start Google Analytics at all. If it is on, Lumolo
uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** (Google LLC) to collect:
- a random identifier created for this installation of the app (app instance ID);
- when the app is opened, how long it is used, and app updates;
- app version, device model, operating-system version and language;
- approximate location (country, region or city) that Google derives from the device's
  IP address; Google Analytics does not store the IP address itself.

We do **not** send names or nicknames, the videos a child watches, favorites, history or any
advertising identifier (IDFA); collection of the device's vendor identifier (IDFV) is
switched off. Advertising storage, ad personalization and Google signals are off, and the
data is not linked to Google Ads or shared with other Google products. We use the statistics
only to understand how many families use Lumolo and which features work, so we can fix
problems and improve the app. The legal basis is the parent's consent (UK GDPR / EU GDPR
Art. 6(1)(a)), which can be withdrawn at any time.

## 3. Information stored only on your device
- Up to four child profiles, each with an optional nickname and an animal picture chosen
  by the parent.
- For each child: favorites, watch history (last 200 items), screen-time usage and
  settings (languages, age range, daily limit, bedtime, hidden or chosen channels).
- A parental PIN, stored as a salted cryptographic hash in the device keychain.
- A copy of the public video list and video thumbnails (deleted automatically after at
  most 25 days without an update, thumbnails after 7 days).

This information never leaves the device (usage statistics, if turned on, are described in
section 2 and never include it) and is not included in device backups where we can prevent it. A parent can delete all of it at any time: **Grown-ups → Delete all data on
this device**, or by deleting the app.

## 4. Network requests the app makes
1. **Our video list** — the app downloads public files from
   `raw.githubusercontent.com` (operated by GitHub, Inc.). The request contains no personal
   information. Like any web server, the host receives the device's IP address as part of
   the connection; we do not log, store or use it. GitHub may log visitor IP
   addresses for security purposes under its own privacy statement
   (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
2. **YouTube** — thumbnails are loaded from YouTube (`i.ytimg.com`), and videos are played
   with **YouTube's embedded player** in privacy-enhanced mode (`youtube-nocookie.com`).
3. **Google Analytics for Firebase** — only if a parent turned on usage statistics
   (section 2). Google processes this data under its data-processing terms and may process it
   outside the UK and EU under standard contractual clauses
   (https://policies.google.com/privacy, https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 5. YouTube API Services
Lumolo uses **YouTube API Services**. By using Lumolo you agree to be bound by the
**YouTube Terms of Service**: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms.
When a video plays, YouTube (Google) may collect information such as the device's IP
address and playback data, and may show its own ads inside the player, under the
**Google Privacy Policy**: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
Videos in Lumolo are designated "made for kids" by their creators; YouTube limits data
collection and disables personalized ads for such content. We do not control YouTube's
data practices. You can revoke YouTube-related access by deleting the app; Lumolo never
accesses any YouTube account, so there is nothing to revoke at
https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

## 6. Children
Lumolo is directed to children. We do not ask children for any information. Usage statistics
are off by default and can be turned on only by a parent, behind the parental gate; they are
used only to run and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling (COPPA, GDPR Art. 8,
UK Age Appropriate Design Code). Settings, outbound links and data deletion are behind a
parental gate and a parent PIN.

## 7. Data retention
Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for 2 months and then deleted automatically.
We keep no other personal data. Video metadata obtained from YouTube is refreshed or deleted
within 30 days on our servers and within 25 days on devices.

## 8. Your rights
Turning off **Share usage statistics** (or **Delete all data on this device**) stops sending
and deletes the app's statistics identifier, so new statistics can no longer be linked to
earlier ones. Because statistics are tied only to that random identifier, we cannot usually
tell which statistics belong to your family; they expire after 2 months. Contact us at
support@samofy.co.uk with any question or request.

Lumolo is operated from the United Kingdom by Samofy Limited. The same practice applies
wherever you live: we never sell or share personal information for advertising, and the only
personal data processed is the optional usage statistics described in section 2, with a
parent's consent (UK GDPR, EU GDPR and other laws, including the CCPA in California). If you have a concern, you can contact us, the UK
Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk) or your local data-protection
authority.

## 9. Changes
We will update this policy here and ask a parent to accept material changes in the app
before continuing.
