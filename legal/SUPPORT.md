# Lumolo — Help & support

Lumolo shows videos only from YouTube channels that a person has reviewed. There is no
search and no account. Everything below is done in the **grown-ups area** (the small lock on
the home screen → answer the question → enter your PIN).

## Contact
Email: [[support email]] — we answer within [[2 business days]].
To report a video or a channel, include the video title and the time where the problem is.
We block confirmed problems for every family as quickly as we can.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I change which languages my family sees?**
Grown-ups area → *Video languages*. Pick a language for all countries, or only specific
countries.

**How do I set a daily limit or a bedtime?**
Grown-ups area → *Screen time*. Changing the device clock does not add time.

**How do I hide a channel?**
Grown-ups area → *Hide channels*, then switch the channel off. This only affects this device.

**I forgot my PIN.**
Delete Lumolo and install it again. This removes the PIN together with favorites, history
and settings (Lumolo keeps no copy anywhere else), and you can set everything up again.

**Why do I sometimes see an ad?**
Videos are played by YouTube's own player, and YouTube may show ads inside it. Lumolo has
no ads of its own and is not allowed to remove YouTube's.

**What data does Lumolo collect?**
None. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

**Videos disappeared after a long time offline.**
Video lists must be refreshed regularly. Connect to the internet and open Lumolo; the list
reloads automatically.
